## Why v2

```
Bitacora
v2 (this file, "codesets_v2.yaml") - guideline rewrite of codesets.yaml (v1), written beside it; codesets.yaml is untouched.
    Sources, both in papers/mammography/:
      USPSTF  Screening for Breast Cancer: USPSTF Recommendation Statement. JAMA 2024;331(22):1918-1930. doi:10.1001/jama.2024.5534
      ASCO    Nahleh Z, et al. Breast Cancer Follow-Up and Surveillance After Primary Treatment: ASCO Guideline Update. J Clin Oncol 2026.
              doi:10.1200/JCO-26-01700 (Table 4 = the recommendations; "Tbl 4 x.y" below refers to its rows)
    What changed against v1 (every change is also a `source:` key on the tag; `python check_codesets.py --md` prints them):
      * the cohort anchor and the label are untouched: `mammography`, `breast_cancer` and `in_situ` resolve to the SAME concept ids as v1,
        so cohort, label, exclusion F2 / F3 and Table 1 are identical and a v1 / v2 run differs only in how features are grouped.
      * every v1 tag name is kept (the notebook reads ANCHOR, LABEL_PROXY_TAGS and codesets[...] by name).
      * v1 defects fixed: drug patterns were anchored with ^ and missed packs and brand forms (ribociclib "{21 (ribociclib ...) } Pack",
        "14 ML pertuzumab ... [Perjeta]", Phesgo, Herceptin Hylecta, Enhertu, Nerlynx, Trodelvy); `mastectomy` held "Mastectomy for
        gynecomastia" (benign); `lymph_node_procedure` also claimed the mastectomy concepts that name axillary nodes, and
        `lumpectomy` + `radiation_therapy` both claimed the IORT applicator code (3 unexpected overlaps in v1, 0 here);
        `radiation_therapy` held the Condition "Stomatitis due to and following radiation therapy"; `breast_reconstruction` held
        "tissue expander for other than breast"; `mammographic_finding` mixed breast density with calcifications and findings.
      * new tags: breast density (USPSTF: supplemental screening is an I statement for dense breasts), germline susceptibility, genetic
        counseling and the ASCO germline panel genes (USPSTF excludes carriers; ASCO Tbl 4 1.3.1 / 3.3), post-mastectomy absence of
        breast (Tbl 4 1.2.2 / 1.3.2: no mammography after bilateral mastectomy), CDK4/6 inhibitors and HER2-targeted drugs (the drugs
        that set ASCO's surveillance intensity, Tbl 4 1.2.1 / 1.3.1), tumor markers, CBC and chemistry panels (Tbl 4 2.1: not
        recommended for surveillance), bone scan and PET (Tbl 4 3.4: not recommended for distant-metastasis surveillance).
      * `receptor_assay` also takes the hormone-receptor / triple-negative tumor conditions (ASCO's intensity groups are defined by subtype).
    Not possible in this extract, so no tag: contrast-enhanced mammography (0 concepts, Tbl 4 3.2); family history of breast cancer
    (an Observation concept, Observation is not loaded); ctDNA (no concept, Tbl 4 2.2); atypical hyperplasia (0 concepts); a
    bilateral-mastectomy concept (only "Bilateral acquired absence of breast"); screening vs surveillance mammography (same concept).
    Not tagged on purpose: plain CT chest / abdomen / pelvis and brain MRI (Tbl 4 3.4) because they are ubiquitous in ED and
    GI / GU care and cannot be told apart from staging scans by concept; LCIS stays inside `in_situ` (the notebook's F3 rule uses it).
    `metastasis` is unchanged: this extract names almost no secondary-neoplasm concepts (1 concept), so the tag stays near-empty.

ORDER IS LOAD-BEARING. tag_events() gives every concept to the FIRST tag that lists it (`group`; at token_level="group" that is also
the NMF token). `groups` (multi_group=True) still lists all tags, so group_mask / label proxies see overlaps. Therefore: specific
tags first, `mammography` after screening / diagnostic, umbrella `antineoplastic_drug` after its finer tags. Do not alphabetise.
Spec shapes (same as v1):
  <tag>: {patterns: [...], vocabularies: [...], domain: ..., exclude: [...], source: "..."}   # name-matched by resolve()
  <tag>: {concept_ids: [...]}                                                                  # literal
PATTERNS ARE UNANCHORED SUBSTRING REGEX joined with "|" (str.contains, case-insensitive) on concept_name of STANDARD concepts.
After editing: python check_codesets.py (matched names per tag, empties, overlaps, v1 diff) and read every changed tag's names.
```
